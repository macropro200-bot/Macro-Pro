Delete your account

Two ways to do it. The first takes about ten seconds.

In the app — immediate

1
Open MACRO PRO and go to Settings.
2
Scroll to the bottom, past Premium.
3
Tap Delete your account and everything in it.
4
Tap it a second time to confirm. That second tap is the point of no return.

Your account, your backed-up programs and your profile photo are removed straight away. Nothing is queued and nobody reviews it.

By email — within 30 days

If you have lost the phone, cannot sign in, or would rather ask in writing, send an email from the address you signed up with. We use that to confirm the account is yours; we cannot delete an account on the word of a different address.

Write the email for me

Acted on within 30 days, usually the same week. You will get a note confirming when it is done.

Two things worth knowing first

  • A purchase is not refunded by deleting an account. Premium is attached to your Google Play account, not to this one — so if you sign up again with the same Google account and press Restore, it comes back. For a refund, ask Google Play.
  • A referral code cannot be reused. Codes are single use, and deleting the account does not release the one you redeemed. If you were given a code and need it again, write to us.

Backups

Deleted data is removed from the live database immediately. Google's own infrastructure backups may hold a copy for up to 30 more days before they roll over. Nothing is read from those backups, and they are not used to restore an account that has been deleted.

Keeping the app, losing the account

You do not have to delete your account to stop the backup. In Settings → Back up automatically, switch it off. The app then works exactly as before with everything stored on the phone alone, and nothing new leaves it.
