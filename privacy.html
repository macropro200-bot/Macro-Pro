Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED 12 SEPTEMBER 2026 (REV 2)

The short version. Every tool in MACRO PRO runs on your phone, and your work is stored there. If you sign in, a copy of it is kept in your account as a safety net — nothing more than that. We do not sell anything to anybody, and we do not track you between apps.

Who we are

MACRO PRO is published by Jagpreet Gautam, an individual developer in India. For anything in this policy, write to macropro200@gmail.com.

Nothing at all until you choose to sign in. The app is fully usable without an account; the only thing you lose is the backup.

How rarely any of it is sent

The app reads and writes your work on the phone, always. The account copy is a backup, and it is treated like one:

  • It is sent when you leave the app, not while you work.
  • At most once every six hours, however much you do.
  • Only if something actually changed. The app compares what it holds against what it last sent and skips the upload when they match.
  • Your name and email are written back at most once a day, not on every launch.
  • Tool-open counts are tallied on the phone and travel with the next backup.

You can switch the backup off entirely in Settings and keep using everything, or press Back up now if you want certainty before handing the phone over.

Advertising

The free version shows adverts through Google AdMob. AdMob uses your device's advertising ID to decide which advert to show and to count it. That identifier is controlled by you: on Android, Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you reset it or turn off personalisation entirely.

Premium removes adverts completely, and with them the ad identifier.

What AdMob does with that data is governed by Google's own policy, not by us.

Purchases

Payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see your card, your name on it, or your billing address. Play tells the app one thing: whether this account has bought the unlock.

What the app does not collect

  • No location. The app has no location permission at all.
  • No contacts, photos, calendar or microphone.
  • No analytics on how you use the tools. Google Analytics is deliberately not enabled.
  • No reading of your files. The app only opens a file you have picked yourself.

Your programs

The programs you generate are your work, and often your customer's part drawing in another form. They are treated accordingly:

  • They live on your phone. That is the copy the app uses, which is why every tool still works with no signal at all.
  • If the backup is on, a copy is kept against your account and is readable only by you. This is enforced by database rules on Google's servers, not just by the app.
  • You can take them with you. Settings → Export writes every program and setting to a single plain file you keep. It is readable text, not a format only this app can open.
  • They are never included in a support email. The diagnostics attached to a bug report carry the app version, your settings and your device model — never the contents of a program.
  • We do not read them, analyse them, or use them for anything.

How long it is kept

For as long as your account exists. Delete the account and everything goes with it — see below. Delete the app without deleting the account and the backup stays, so that signing in on a new phone still restores your work.

If you never sign in, nothing is kept anywhere but your phone, and uninstalling the app removes it. Export first if you want to keep it.

Your date of birth

Asked for once, when you create an account, and required. It is stored with your account and a copy is kept on the phone so the app still works with no signal.

It is used for two things and nothing else:

  • To say happy birthday on the day.
  • To confirm you are old enough to use the app. MACRO PRO is a tool for people who operate machinery and accounts are for ages 16 and over. If you are younger, every tool and all 46 lessons still work without an account — you just do not get the backup.

It is never used for advertising, never shared, and never sold. Change or clear it in Settings, or delete your account and it goes with everything else.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Delete your account and everything in it. Two taps. It removes your account, your backed-up programs and your profile photo immediately.

Or by email: write to macropro200@gmail.com from the address you signed up with. Deleted within 30 days, usually the same week. See the account deletion page.

Your rights

Wherever you are, you can ask for a copy of what is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to macropro200@gmail.com. If you are in the UK or EU, the lawful bases are consent for the optional account and legitimate interest for keeping the app working and secure.

Children

MACRO PRO is a professional tool for machinists and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and it will be removed.

Where the data sits

On Google Cloud servers in India (asia-south2). Google may process it elsewhere under their own terms; see the Firebase privacy documentation.

Security

  • Everything travels over HTTPS. The app refuses unencrypted connections outright.
  • Database rules on the server allow you to read and write only your own record.
  • Passwords are never seen by the app or by us — Firebase Authentication handles them.
  • No system is perfect, and this one is built by one person. If you find a hole, please tell us rather than anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the date at the top changes and the app will say so on next launch. Material changes will not be applied retroactively to data already collected.
